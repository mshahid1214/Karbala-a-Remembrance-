A note on this site

Made with restraint, on purpose

Shia tradition, like the wider Islamic tradition, is careful about depicting the Prophet and the Imams. Nothing on this site attempts to show their faces. Instead you'll find geometric ornament, calligraphy, and the Panja the open hand that has stood for the Ahl al-Bayt on processional standards for centuries.

One page is different

The tribute page uses your camera

Only the tribute page asks for camera access, to detect an open hand and light a candle on screen. That video is read by your browser and never leaves your device every other page is plain reading, with no camera involved at all.

كل يوم عاشوراء، وكل أرض كربلاء

A saying carried through generations of mourners: every day is Ashura, every land is Karbala meaning the stand Hussain took against tyranny was never meant to close with the tenth century's tenth of Muharram. It is retold every year so it stays a live question, not a closed story.
